How to Automatically Capture & View Screenshots for These Screens

  1. Connect your Android phone (with USB debugging) or start an Android Emulator in Android Studio.
  2. Run python capture_screens.py — it automatically tracks whatever screen you navigate to and snaps a screenshot directly matching the screen name.
  3. Refresh this page, and all screenshots will appear directly above each screen title! You can also click any image to view it full screen.
